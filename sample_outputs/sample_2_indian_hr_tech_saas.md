# Research Report: Find 5 Indian B2B SaaS startups in HR tech and summarize their positioning.

## Executive Summary
The Indian HR Tech B2B SaaS market is sharply stratified into enterprise conglomerates, mid-market tech companies, and SMBs. Darwinbox dominates enterprise accounts competing with Workday; Keka leads the mid-market with intuitive payroll and UX; Zimyo targets SMBs via embedded employee financial benefits; greytHR serves long-tail compliance; and inFeedo leverages AI sentiment to prevent employee churn.

## Key Findings
### 1. Darwinbox: Enterprise Conglomerate Positioning (Confidence: High)
Darwinbox targets large-scale enterprises with 1,000+ employees (e.g., Adani, Mahindra), positioning itself as a modular, mobile-first alternative to legacy ERPs like SAP and Workday across Asia [1].

### 2. Keka: Mid-Market & SME Delight (Confidence: High)
Keka focuses on tech-forward mid-market companies (100–2,000 employees), differentiating on automated statutory payroll compliance (PF/ESI/TDS) and zero-friction employee self-service [1].

### 3. Zimyo: SMB FinTech & Benefits Integration (Confidence: High)
Zimyo addresses smaller businesses (20–300 staff) by combining core HR workflows with embedded financial wellness tools such as on-demand earned wage access and insurance [1].

### 4. greytHR & inFeedo: Compliance Scale and AI Engagement (Confidence: High)
greytHR maintains dominance in high-volume payroll compliance for established SMBs, while inFeedo specializes in conversational AI (Amber) to detect employee disengagement and flight risk [1].
